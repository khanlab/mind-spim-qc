Align the template

The template is aligned to the image only to start each landmark view near its place: a few hundred µm off is fine. Magenta: template edges; yellow: template brain outline. Click a view to move the slices.

Align on a channel that shows the anatomy (nuclear stain or autofluorescence), not a sparse stain. Left–right cannot be judged from the fit: a mirrored orientation (e.g. LAI for RAI) fits almost as well, so take the orientation from the acquisition.

    Placements are also kept in this browser and come back when you open the same protocol, image, rater and session.

    Click a view to move the cursor; wheel moves through slices (shift: faster); double-click centres the view. Keys: Space place · A absent · D damaged · C centre · N/P next/previous · +/− field.
