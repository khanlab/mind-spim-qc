Align the template

An affine alignment of the template (ABAv3) to this image. For landmarks it only starts each view near its place: a few hundred µm off is fine. Magenta: template edges; yellow: template brain outline. Click a view to move the slices.

Align on a channel that shows the anatomy (nuclear stain or autofluorescence), not a sparse stain. Left–right cannot be judged from the fit: a mirrored orientation (e.g. LAI for RAI) fits almost as well, so take the orientation from the acquisition.
